## In a paragraph or more, detail your project. What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?

I was thinking of an alarm clock that has a daily planner suggestions built in. Say if I am feeling like there a lot of noises in my life or if when the mind is a loud place so this can help. People waking up from whatever they choose to wake up to, songs, ambient, it will be mp3 uploaded for their desired tracks. I hope it to make it open sourced so anyone can customize it.

It will be a Python app. The user opens it, sets their alarm time like any other alarm, and picks what music they want to wake up to, in an audio file or MP3 section for their desired tracks. When the alarm goes off at the set time, it plays that track, then shows the daily planner suggestions, the morning list, plus the meditation and breathing tricks. The app will have tabs for it, Alarms, Music, Planner, and Breathing, so the user can set their alarms and pick their music. We do not know exactly how the UI will be built yet, but that is the layout.
